 AI RESUME & JOB/INTERNSHIP MATCHING PLATFORM 
SkillMatch AI: An Artificial Intelligence-Based Resume Parsing and Job/Internship 
Matching Platform for Graduating Students 
Introduction / Background of the Study 
Every year, graduating and OJT-bound students face the same recurring problem: matching their skills 
and qualifications to the right job or internship opening. Traditional job hunting relies on students 
manually browsing listings and recruiters manually screening piles of resumes, a process that is slow, 
inconsistent, and prone to overlooking qualified candidates simply because a resume was not formatted 
the way a recruiter expected. 
SkillMatch AI addresses this by using natural language processing (NLP) to parse resumes, extract 
structured data such as skills, education, and experience, and match that data against job or internship 
postings using a similarity-scoring or recommendation model. Instead of a static keyword search, the 
system ranks and recommends the most relevant opportunities to each student, while giving companies 
a shortlist of pre-screened, qualified applicants. 
The project draws directly from the researchers' own OJT experience, where the mismatch between a 
student's actual skill set and the tasks assigned during internship placement is a recurring and 
observable problem worth solving with technology. 
Statement of the Problem 
The study aims to address the following problems: 
1. Students often apply to jobs or internships that do not match their actual skill sets, leading to poor 
placement outcomes and wasted application effort. 
2. Manual resume screening by recruiters and OJT coordinators is time-consuming and inconsistent, 
and may overlook qualified applicants due to formatting or keyword mismatches. 
3. There is no centralized, intelligent platform that both parses a student's resume and actively 
recommends suitable opportunities based on that data. 
4. Existing job boards rely on static filters (e.g., location, job title) rather than deeper skill-based 
matching. 
Objectives of the Study 
General Objective: To design, develop, and evaluate an AI-powered web platform that parses student 
resumes and matches them to relevant job or internship postings using machine learning-based 
recommendation techniques. 
Specific Objectives: 
• To develop a resume upload and parsing module that extracts structured data (skills, education, 
experience) using NLP techniques. 
• To design a job/internship posting module for employers or OJT coordinators to list available 
opportunities. 
• To implement a matching or recommendation algorithm that scores and ranks opportunities based 
on resume-to-posting similarity. 
• To build a dashboard for students to track applications and for employers to view ranked, 
shortlisted candidates. 
• To evaluate the accuracy and relevance of the AI matching results against manual screening as a 
baseline. 
Scope and Limitations 
The system will support resume upload (PDF/DOCX), automated parsing, job/internship posting 
management, and AI-based matching and ranking. It will be limited to text-based resume data (not 
scanned images without OCR), a defined and pre-agreed set of skill/keyword categories, and a 
moderate-sized pilot dataset of job postings and student resumes (e.g., partnering with the school's OJT 
office). The study will not cover payroll, contract management, or legal hiring compliance features, and 
initial matching accuracy will be based on a proof-of-concept model rather than production-scale 
training data. 
Significance of the Study 
• Students: Receive personalized, ranked job/internship recommendations instead of manually sifting 
through listings. 
• Employers / OJT Coordinators: Save time by receiving a pre-screened, ranked shortlist of qualified 
applicants. 
• School / OJT Office: Gains a tool to better track placement outcomes and skill-job alignment across 
cohorts. 
• Future Researchers: Provides a working baseline and dataset structure for future resume-matching 
or HR-tech studies.
